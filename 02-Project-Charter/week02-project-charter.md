# Project Charter Draft: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System Replacement
**Consultant:** Shawn Coy
**Role:** Associate Consultant, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** September 15, 2026
---
## Project Title
RCHN Patient and Scheduling System Replacement
---
## Project Sponsor
Chief Operating Officer (COO) of RCHN
---
## Business Need
RCHN's patient intake system that is currently in use has grown outdated. Scheduling is delayed and an alarming number of patients and workers across all six clinics are reporting issues with the system that hinder ease of use. Implementing a new system will make scheduling visits easier for patients and will improve management of visits, patient records, and other data much easier for staff.
---
## Purpose and High-Level Scope
The purpose of the project is to replace the patient intake and scheduling system with a new streamlined platform that improves ease of use for all RCHN employees and their patients across all six of their clinics. All clinic workers will be trained on the new system, and all existing patient records will be securely migrated over to ensure a smooth transition with zero interruption to daily appointments.
---
## Success Criteria
Staff can check in patients and schedule appointments across all six clinics without the system crashing or freezing. All existing patient profiles, medical history, and upcoming appointments are visible in the new system with no missing info. Clinic workers can log in and complete basic scheduling tasks on their own without needing immediate IT support.
