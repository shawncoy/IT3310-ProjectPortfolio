# Product Backlog: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System
**Consultant:** Shawn Coy
**Role:** Associate Consultant, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Regional Care Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** October 20, 2026
---
## Product Backlog

### Story 1
**User Story:** As a Clinic intake coordinator, I want a patient registration and scheduling system, so that Patients can check in online, update their medical history, and reduce wait times.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Given a new patient is accessing the portal, when they complete and submit their details, then the system saves the record to the database and notifies the intake coordinator.
**Story Points:** 13 points
**MoSCoW Priority:** Must Have

### Story 2
**User Story:** As a Primary care physician, I want A clean digital dashboard for patient charts, so that I can quickly review patient vitals, clinical notes and lab results during consultations without having to go through paper files.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Given a physician is viewing a patient profile, when they click on the lab results tab, then the system displays all recent lab reports sorted chronologically with abnormal readings highlighted.
**Story Points:** 8 points
**MoSCoW Priority:** Must Have

### Story 3
**User Story:** As a Pharmacy technician, I want An automated e-prescription and refill queue, so that Orders sent over by doctors go directly to the pharmacy and no refill requests are lost.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Given a doctor signs off on a new prescription, when they send it through, then the pharmacy receives the electronic order within seconds.
**Story Points:** 8 points
**MoSCoW Priority:** Should Have

### Story 4
**User Story:** As a Medical billing specialist, I want a structured billing and insurance verification system, so that I can be more efficient in navigating medical records to verify insurance, create and submit claims, and monitor bills and inform patients.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Given a patient has verified their insurance eligibility, when I add an eligibility note in the system, then the system will show the eligibility details to all RCHN workers and the patient. Given a submitted insurance claim has been processed with an outstanding balance, when I update the patient's account status, then the system will automatically calculate the remaining amount and generate a billing notification for the patient.
**Story Points:** 8 points
**MoSCoW Priority:** Should Have

### Story 5
**User Story:** As a Community health worker, I want a mobile app that can track home visits and log notes offline, so that I can update patient files and care plans when there is no cell service available.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Given I change a patient's address or contact information in the app, when I save the updated information, then it updates the main database immediately for all clinic workers to view.
**Story Points:** 13 points
**MoSCoW Priority:** Won't Have This Time
---
## Prioritization Rationale
### Must Have Justification
I marked the digital dashboard for primary care physicians as must have. It achieves the goal of making the physicians' jobs much simpler because there will be no need for paper records, which saves a lot of time during appointments and keeps patient care running smoothly without the possibility of losing important notes.

### Won't Have Rationale
I deferred the mobile app for community health workers. It is not currently being prioritized because of the extra resources and time that it will take to develop. An alternative that fits the timeline of the project could be a system that can be accessed through mobile device browsers, as it removes the need for a separate mobile app development team.
---
## Backlog Reflection
### Persona Connection
For user story 2, the Week 3 persona research highlighted how much time primary care physicians waste digging through messy paper charts and notes during visits. This story directly addresses that need by setting up a clean digital dashboard so doctors can pull up vitals and labs instantly while talking to patients.
### Scope Connection
For user story 3, the Week 5 scope outlined the need to streamline pharmacy workflows and handle prescriptions more efficiently. This backlog item takes that high-level scope and gives developers specific acceptance criteria so they know exactly how e-prescriptions and refill requests need to route and pop up in the queue.
