# Project Plan Foundation: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System
**Consultant:** Shawn Coy
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Project Manager, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** October 6, 2026
---
## Scope
### In Scope, Phase One
Patient intake workflow standardization across all 6 clinics
Appointment scheduling system replacement
Staff training for the new system at all six locations
Initial rollout support for 90 days post launch

### Out of Scope, Phase One
Patient-facing online scheduling portal
Billing system integration with new scheduling data
Executive reporting dashboard for clinic leadership
Mobile application for clinic staff

### Scope Justification
There is no need for a mobile application for clinic staff. Typically the staff members will be using RCHN-issued devices to navigate the new system rather than their personal mobile devices. A mobile device also will make the project much more complex by requiring a separate development and testing timeline for the mobile application. That will increase the budget and also cause the project schedule to be pushed back a fair amount.
---
## SMART Objectives
### Objective 1
By 1 October 2027, medical assistants will reduce the average length of patient intake encounters by 20% thanks to the new system. System time logs will verify. 
**Connection to Product Vision:** This objective is informed by the product vision because it directly measures the efficiency gained from making patient records easier to navigate.

### Objective 2
By 1 November, 2027, medical assistants will be able to access a patient's core medical history in 3 clicks or fewer, as verified by system usability testing.
**Connection to Product Vision:** This objective is informed by the product vision because reducing the physical steps required to find information directly solves the problem of navigating disorganized patient records.

---
## Planning Constraints
### Constraint 1
The project is restricted to a software-only implementation, meaning no new hardware or mobile devices will be purchased for clinic staff.
**Effect on Planning:** This constraint affects scope and cost because the team must design the new interface to run efficiently on the clinic's existing computers without requiring an additional budget for hardware upgrades.
### Constraint 2
Phase 1 of the rollout strictly excludes the patient-facing online scheduling portal and billing system integration.**Effect on Planning:** This constraint affects scope and schedule because it narrows the focus of Phase 1 entirely to internal intake workflows, which reduces the immediate development time and keeps the project from becoming too complex.

---
## Assumptions
We are assuming that RCHN's current hardware and network infrastructure will be able to support the new software with no issue. If this is wrong, then we will have to pivot away from a software-only implementation and will have to adjust the scope and budget and timeline of our project to include hardware upgrades.

We are assuming that all the old patient records can be automatically transferred into the new system without any data loss. If this is wrong, then we will have to pivot our resource plan and adjust our project timeline to account for the IT team and clinic staff having to manually enter that data.
---
## Planning Foundation Synthesis
I decided to leave the staff mobile application out of the project scope. This was informed by our Product Vision, which focuses on helping medical assistants organize data efficiently at their main front-desk workstations. The additional judgment I made was that building a mobile app would add too much technical complexity and negatively affect our schedule. Before this is treated as final, it still needs confirmation from the RCHN clinic managers to ensure their current patient intake workflow doesn't actually require mobile devices.
