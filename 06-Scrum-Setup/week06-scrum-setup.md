## Agile Delivery Fit
I think the design of the interface would benefit greatly from iterative delivery. In this case, iterative delivery will allow us to seek constant feedback from the clinic workers, assistants, and other important stakeholders to make sure the UI is as fine tuned to their preferences as possible. Inversely, system security will need much stronger control than the UI design.
---
## Scrum Team
**Product Owner:** NovaTech Product Manager
**Scrum Master:** NovaTech Implementation Lead
**Developers:** Software engineers at NovaTech

### Rationale
The Product Owner will be the NovaTech Product Manager because they are responsible for maximizing the value of the new medical assistant interface and managing the backlog. The Scrum Master will be the NovaTech Implementation Lead because their role is to facilitate the sprints and keep everyone on track. The Developers will be the software engineers at NovaTech since they are doing the hands-on technical work for the system.

## Sprint 1 Plan
**Sprint Goal:** Begin outlining and building the updated scheduling workflow using existing intake and scheduling data to assist.
**Course Planning Limit:** 25 points

### Selected Backlog Items
- Scheduling Workflow Prototype, High priority, 13 points
- Intake Form Standardization, High priority, 8 points
- Legacy Data Cleanup Review, Low priority, 3 points

**Total Course Planning Points Selected:** 24
---
## Sprint Planning Reflection
I selected these 3 tasks for my sprint 1 plan because they tie directly into my goal of building the new workflow using our existing data. I grabbed the high-priority prototype and intake form tasks first since the team really needs to figure out the standard fields before building the actual interface. Tacking on the low-priority data cleanup review brought me to 24 points, which makes the most of the 25-point limit without going over. I intentionally held off on actually moving the patient data over because that should definitely wait until the clinic staff reviews and approves the basic prototype.
---
## Scrum Master Reflection
### Obstacle and Response
The team might realize the scheduling prototype is way more complicated than we thought and we might not finish it. The Scrum Master could help by facilitating a quick meeting for the developers to figure out if they can scale back the prototype's features for this sprint, letting the team decide how to adjust rather than telling them what to cut.

### Preparing for Week 7
Having to stick to one clear sprint goal this week really helped focus the task selections. When I build the product backlog next week, I can use that same mindset to group related tasks together and prioritize them based on what delivers the most value to the medical assistants right away.
