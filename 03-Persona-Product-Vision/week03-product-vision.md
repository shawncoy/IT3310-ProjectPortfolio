# User Persona and Product Vision: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System
**Consultant:** Shawn Coy
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Product Manager, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** September 22, 2026
---
## User Persona
**Name:** Harold
**Role:** Medical Assistant
**Years in Role:** 1-3 years
**Tech Comfort Level:** High, learns new software quickly
---
### Primary Work Goal
To organize patient data efficiently and without error
---
### Main Frustration with the Current System
Current system is too difficult to navigate quickly due to a cluttered and outdated UI
compared to other tools they have used in the past.
---
### What Would Make Their Work Better
A complete UI overhaul to make it easier to navigate patient records and locate specific data.
---
## Product Vision Statement
For medical assistants who struggle to organize patient data efficiently, a system and ui overhaul will make it easier to navigate patient records and locate data.
