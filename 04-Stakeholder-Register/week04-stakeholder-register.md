## Engagement Strategy Reflection
### Highest Priority Stakeholder
The RCHN Hospital Administrator needs the most focus because they are the main sponsor with high interest and influence. They control the budget and decide the clinic's efficiency goals. If we ignore their updates, they could pull funding or reject the final software and ruin the project.

### Risk of Omission
I thought about adding the external software vendor but left them out since our Implementation Lead is already handling that technical relationship. I would add the vendor later if we realize we need custom API work to connect the new system with old patient records, which would force us to work with them directly.
